About VA Launchpad

Coaching for new virtual assistants

Read moreShow less

VA Launchpad is a coaching service by CathiesNest Digital for people starting out as virtual assistants. We offer friendly, practical guidance with clear prices, so you always know what you are paying for and what to expect.

Coaching led by Catherine of CathiesNest Digital.

Who this is for

  • Beginners exploring a virtual assistant career
  • Career shifters looking for flexible, remote work
  • Students and fresh graduates building their first skills
  • Anyone who wants a stronger CV and portfolio

Looking for tools to run your VA work? Try StartOps, also from CathiesNest Digital.

Terms and agreement

Simple, fair terms

Read termsHide terms

Please read these before you book. They are written to be short and clear. By ticking the agreement box when you request a session, you accept them and agree to pay for the service you requested.

  1. What you are bookingThe service you choose from the price list. The date, time and format are agreed with you by email before the session.
  2. PaymentYou agree to pay the listed price for the service you requested. Payment is by GCash or GoTyme Bank. Payment details are sent to you by email after you request a session. Please send proof of payment so your booking can be confirmed.
  3. PricesPrices are in Philippine pesos. The price confirmed in writing before you pay is the price you pay. Later price changes do not affect a confirmed booking.
  4. Rescheduling and cancellingPlease tell us at least 24 hours before your session to move it once at no charge. A late cancellation or no-show may mean the session is forfeited. If Catherine has to cancel, you can choose a new time or a full refund of what you paid for that session.
  5. No guaranteesWe do not guarantee employment, clients or income. See the disclaimer above.
  6. Your partGive accurate information and take part honestly. Your progress depends largely on your own effort.
  7. PrivacyYour name, email, CV, portfolio and anything you share are used only to deliver your service. We do not sell them or share them without your permission, and we handle them in line with the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012. Ask us any time to delete what you sent.
  8. Your own workYou must own, or have permission to share, anything you send us for review. Coaching is guidance and is not legal, tax or financial advice.
  9. Respectful conductWe treat you with respect and ask the same in return. Either side may end a session that becomes abusive.
  10. Your rightsNothing here limits any rights you have under Philippine law as a consumer. Questions about these terms? Email cathiesnest@gmail.com before you book.
